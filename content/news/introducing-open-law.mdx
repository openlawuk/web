# Introducing Open Law

Open Law establishes the public standards and directory layer for legal practice data portability. Inspired by Open Banking, it separates **open rails** from commercial connectivity so clients can authorise the apps they choose.

## What we're building

- A **Directory** trust anchor for participants and software credentials
- **Dynamic Client Registration** so apps connect to firm endpoints with cryptographic identity
- **Read/Write APIs** on a vendor-neutral ontology for matters, documents, and financials
- **Open Data** for public discovery of participants and published prices

## Who it's for

Firms host practice data. Apps build client-facing and firm-facing software. Technical providers implement the APIs. Regulators gain transparency through client-visible data boundaries and conformance attestation.

The specifications are Apache-2.0 and maintained in public GitHub repositories — independent of any single vendor.
